Futly privacy notice

Effective 26 October 2026

Futly helps customers find turf availability, book a pitch, and view bookings linked to their account. This notice explains how the customer booking app uses information.

Account and booking information

We use your name, email address and account identifier to sign you in and link your bookings across devices. Booking records include the contact name and phone number you enter, team name, venue, pitch, date, time, booking status, and payment records recorded by the venue. Email and password authentication is processed through Supabase Auth.

Sign in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares basic identity information with our authentication provider, Supabase: your Google account identifier, email address, name and profile information, which may include a profile picture. Futly uses your verified identity to sign you in and connect bookings to your profile. Futly does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts or Calendar. Google API access and refresh tokens are not retained by Futly's booking application; Supabase account sessions are used to keep you signed in.

Cookies and browser storage

Essential cookies support sign-in, session security and booking requests. If you start Google sign-in while booking, the unfinished slot and contact details are temporarily kept in that browser tab's session storage. Futly reads and removes that draft on return, and ignores drafts older than one hour. Signing out clears the app's displayed account and booking history.

Service providers and venues

Futly uses Cloudflare to host and serve the app, Supabase for authentication and stored booking records, and Resend to deliver account emails. These providers process information needed to operate those services, including technical request and security logs. The venue receiving your booking can access the booking and contact details needed to manage it and record payments. Google processes Google sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Storage and your choices

Account and booking records remain stored to provide account history and venue operations. Signing out or cancelling a booking does not delete those records. You can browse availability without signing in, choose email sign-in instead of Google, and manage Futly's Google connection in your Google account settings. Disconnecting Google does not itself delete your Futly account or venue records.

Requests require identity verification; booking or payment records may need to be retained for venue records or legal obligations. Contact the venue directly about booking changes and payments.
